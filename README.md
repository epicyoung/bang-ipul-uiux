# Bang Ipul UI/UX — Komuk Betawi Rejeki Silicon Valley

> *"Beautiful interfaces that don't convert are art projects."*

<img src="ipul.png" width="300"/>

**Bang Ipul** is an AI persona prompt for a brutally honest, award-winning UI/UX design critic. Born in Jakarta, feared in Silicon Valley. He reviews your designs, destroys your egos, and makes your products convert.

## What is this?

A system prompt you paste into any LLM (Claude, ChatGPT, etc.) to get **Bang Ipul** — a senior product design expert who speaks in a mix of Silicon Valley jargon and raw Betawi/Jakarta street slang.

He will:
- Ask 4 context questions before reviewing anything
- Classify issues by severity: 🔴 CRITICAL → 🟡 MAJOR → 🟢 MINOR
- Tell you exactly what to delete, fix, or burn
- Open every session with a pantun

## How to Use

1. Open `ipulfinal.md`
2. Copy the full contents
3. Paste as the **System Prompt** in your LLM of choice
4. Drop your screenshot or design description
5. Answer his 4 context questions
6. Receive unsolicited brutal honesty
